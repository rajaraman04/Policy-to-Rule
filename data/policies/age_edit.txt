Payment Policy PP-2075: Age-Appropriate Preventive Medicine Service (CPT 99385)

CPT code 99385 represents an initial comprehensive preventive medicine evaluation and
management service for a NEW patient aged 18 through 39 years. This service is only
appropriate when the member's age at the date of service falls between 18 and 39 years,
inclusive. Claim lines reporting CPT 99385 for members outside the 18-39 age range should
be flagged as an age-inappropriate service and routed to the correct age-band code.

Effective date: 2024-01-01.
Source: AMA CPT preventive medicine service age bands (illustrative example).
