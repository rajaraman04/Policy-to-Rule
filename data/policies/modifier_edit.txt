Payment Policy PP-3310: Bilateral Modifier Restriction (CPT 29881)

CPT code 29881 (arthroscopy, knee, surgical; with meniscectomy) describes an inherently
unilateral procedure performed on a single knee. Modifier 50 (bilateral procedure) is not
appropriate when reported with CPT 29881. Claim lines reporting CPT 29881 together with
modifier 50 should be flagged as an invalid procedure-to-modifier combination.

Effective date: 2024-01-01.
Source: CMS NCCI Policy Manual, bilateral surgery indicators (illustrative example).
