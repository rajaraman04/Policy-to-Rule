Clinical Practice Guideline - Colorectal Cancer Screening:
The USPSTF recommends screening for colorectal cancer in average-risk adults beginning at
age 45 and continuing through age 75. This 2021 recommendation lowered the starting age
from 50 to 45. Screening initiated before age 45 falls outside the recommended starting
age for average-risk individuals. Claim lines reporting a screening colonoscopy
(HCPCS G0121) for a member younger than 45 years should be flagged as below the
guideline-recommended screening start age.
