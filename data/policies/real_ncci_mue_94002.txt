Policy - Medically Unlikely Edit (MUE):
A Medically Unlikely Edit sets the maximum number of units of service (UOS) that a
provider may report for a given HCPCS/CPT code for the same beneficiary on the same
date of service. When the units reported on a claim line exceed the code's MUE value,
the units on that line are denied.

Worked example from the manual:
CPT code 94002 (ventilation assist and management, initial day) has an MUE value of 1
and an MUE Adjudication Indicator (MAI) of 2 - an absolute per-day edit. Reporting more
than 1 unit of CPT 94002 for the same beneficiary on the same date of service is
contrary to correct coding policy, because a single date of service cannot represent
two "initial" days of ventilation management.
