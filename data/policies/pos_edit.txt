Payment Policy PP-4120: Place of Service Restriction (CPT 99213)

CPT code 99213 (office or other outpatient visit for the evaluation and management of an
established patient) is payable in outpatient settings. It is not payable when reported
with place of service 21 (inpatient hospital), because inpatient evaluation and management
is described by a different code family. Claim lines reporting CPT 99213 with place of
service 21 should be flagged as a place-of-service conflict.

Effective date: 2024-01-01.
Source: CMS Place of Service code set and E/M billing guidelines (illustrative example).
