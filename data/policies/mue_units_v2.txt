Payment Policy PP-1042: Medically Unlikely Edits (MUE) - Skin Tag Removal (CPT 11200)

CPT code 11200 (removal of skin tags, up to and including 15 lesions) is subject to a
Medically Unlikely Edit. A single claim line for CPT 11200 must not exceed 10 units of
service for the same member on the same date of service. Claim lines reporting more than
10 units for CPT 11200 should be flagged for review, as they exceed the medically
reasonable maximum number of units for this procedure. Providers billing above this limit
must submit supporting documentation for manual clinical review.

Effective date: 2025-01-01. (Supersedes the 2024-01-01 revision, which used a 15-unit limit.)
Source: CMS National Correct Coding Initiative (NCCI) MUE policy manual (illustrative example).
